Home base

Midvale, UT 84047

Our office is at 75 East Fort Union Blvd, Midvale, UT 84047, in Salt Lake County. Dispatch for the whole service area runs from here, and the line is answered 24/7. Midvale is bordered by Murray to the north, Cottonwood Heights to the east, Sandy to the south and the Jordan River to the west.

Inside the city we work in Old Town, Fort Union, Bingham Junction and Jordan Bluffs, along with every street in between. Four sewer districts serve different parts of Midvale, so who owns the main depends on the street. If a blockage is out in the main, we can tell you which district to call.
