The basics

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A drain cable punches a hole through a clog. It leaves the grease, scale and sludge on the pipe wall, which is why the line often clogs again within weeks.

Hydro jetting sends water through a hose and a nozzle at high pressure, with jets that spin and scour the full circumference of the pipe. The result is a line scrubbed back toward its original diameter, so a 4-inch drain flows like a 4-inch drain again.

When a clogged line needs jetting right now

A single slow drain does not need a jetter. These signs usually do:

  • A main line that backs up again within weeks of being snaked
  • A restaurant or commercial kitchen line packed with grease
  • Roots that grow back into the line every season
  • Scale and sludge from decades of use in an older house
  • Sewage reaching a floor drain with the cable stopped by heavy buildup

Cable or jetter

Snaking versus hydro jetting

Both clear a clog. They are not interchangeable, and a good plumber picks the tool from what is in the pipe.

ComparedDrain cableHydro jetter
Works well onSoft clogs and a single blockageGrease, scale, sludge and root hairs
Leaves behindCoating on the pipe wallA clean pipe wall
Reach50 to 100 feet in a main lineLong runs from a cleanout
RisksCan punch through weak pipeWrong pressure on damaged pipe can worsen it
Best forA fast fix for a first clogRepeat clogs and heavy buildup

For a first-time clog in a sink or tub, drain cleaning with a cable is usually the right tool.

What to expect on a jetting visit

The visit follows a short order, and the camera decides whether we jet at all.

  1. 1

    Camera first

    A camera shows what is in the line and whether the pipe can take pressure.

  2. 2

    Set up at the cleanout

    We feed the hose through a cleanout or a pulled toilet, not through the wall.

  3. 3

    Jet the line

    The nozzle works forward to cut the blockage, then back to scour the wall.

  4. 4

    Check with the camera

    A second pass shows the result and any damage the buildup was hiding.

  5. 5

    Talk through the findings

    If the pipe is cracked or collapsed, we explain what that means for repair.

Honest limits

When hydro jetting is not the answer

Jetting is a cleaning tool. It cannot rebuild a pipe that has failed.

Jetting will not help when

  • The pipe is collapsed or badly offset
  • The line is Orangeburg or heavily deteriorated
  • A tree root has pushed through a broken joint
  • A solid object, not buildup, is the blockage

What we recommend instead

  • A camera to see the damage
  • Lining or replacement under sewer line repair
  • Cutting out roots and repairing the joint
  • Retrieving the object or pulling the toilet

If the camera finds broken pipe, we will point you to sewer line repair rather than jet a line that cannot hold.

In Midvale

Older lines and who owns the pipe

Midvale's historic downtown and Old Town streets date to the mining and railroad era, and older drain pipe there is more likely to be clay or cast iron with scale built up on the walls. That is exactly the case where a camera comes first, since a thin or cracked pipe may not take pressure.

The lateral from your building to the main typically belongs to the property owner, and four sewer districts serve different parts of town. If the blockage is in the main in the street, jetting your line will not help, and we will say so after the camera pass.
