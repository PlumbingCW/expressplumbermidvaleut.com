Midvale homes

Why Midvale plumbing fails when it does

Midvale is a city of mixed ages. Old Town grew up around mining and the railroad from the 1870s, while the Fort Union area began as farmland and joined the city later. The city's own water plan calls many of its distribution pipes old and undersized, and it replaces leaking galvanized service laterals with copper or poly.

A damp patch between the meter and your house is a job for leak detection, which shows whether the leak is on your side of the line.

Lateral ownership matters here. The city says maintenance of water and sewer laterals typically falls to the property owner, not the municipality. A leak in the street main is a different story, and Midvale Public Works takes those calls at 801-567-7235, with an on-call employee reached by pressing 0.

Hardness is the other local factor. Tap water bought from Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District averaged 182.4 mg/L total hardness in its 2025 report, which the USGS classes as very hard. That is about 10.7 grains per gallon, enough to scale a tank over the years. West-side homes run on city wells, so ask Midvale Public Utilities for the annual water quality report. If scale is a concern, see water heater repair and water softener installation.

Common calls

Problems that send people to an emergency plumber

Four situations account for most late-night calls.

Burst or leaking pipes

A split supply line can put gallons on the floor in minutes. Burst pipe repair starts with stopping the water and ends with a permanent fix.

Backed-up drains and sewers

When a tub fills with sewage, the blockage is usually in the main line. Drain cleaning clears it, and hydro jetting handles heavy buildup.

No hot water or a leaking tank

A tank that drips at the base is usually at the end of its life. We can look at both repair and replacement options.

Overflowing toilets and failed fixtures

A toilet that keeps running or a faucet that will not shut off can often be fixed the same day. See toilet repair for the usual causes.
