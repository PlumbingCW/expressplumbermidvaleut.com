The area

Where Fort Union and Union Park fit in Midvale

Fort Union Boulevard is one of the main east-west roads through Midvale, and the Fort Union and Union Park areas grew up along it. Local history describes this part of town as farmland that joined the city later, in contrast with the older core near Main Street.

Today it is a mix of single-family streets, townhomes and apartments, with shops and offices along the boulevard, including the Shops at Fort Union. We describe the area by its roads because the city does not publish a boundary for either name.

For older and mixed-use buildings, see Old Town and Main Street. For newer multi-unit buildings, see Bingham Junction.

Hard water and your water heater: what to check

Scale builds up inside a tank over the years. These checks help you catch trouble before the tank fails.

  1. 1

    Note the age

    Find the date on the tank label. An older tank has had more years of scale.

  2. 2

    Listen for popping

    Rumbling or popping during heating can mean scale on the bottom of the tank.

  3. 3

    Look for moisture

    A wet base or rust stains on the floor need a call the same day.

  4. 4

    Ask about options

    Repair, replacement and a softener are different answers to different problems.

Our water heater repair and water heater replacement pages explain each path, and water softener installation covers treating the water itself.

Which sewer district serves your address

Four sewer providers serve different parts of Midvale: Midvale City, Cottonwood Improvement District, Midvalley Improvement District and Sandy Suburban Improvement District. Which one serves a street in this area depends on the address, and we could not find a public map that settles it for every lot.

Midvale says the lateral from a building to the main typically belongs to the property owner. If a blockage sits in the main in the street, the district for your address handles it. A camera pass shows which side the problem is on, and our sewer line cleaning page explains the clearing.

Building types

Common plumbing setups along Fort Union Boulevard

The right first move depends on the building, so here are the usual ones.

Single-family homes

Find the main shutoff, the water heater and the sewer cleanout before you need them. A shutoff that has never been turned may be stiff.

Townhomes and condos

Check which lines the association holds. Fixtures and the heater are often yours, while shared drains and risers often are not.

Apartments

Report a leak to management in writing and photograph the damage. Management decides who may enter.

Shops and offices

Restrooms, break-room sinks and water heaters see steady use. Read your lease to see who pays for plumbing work.

Permits when you replace a water heater

The city says water heater replacement requires a building permit and inspections, and only the contractor on record schedules inspections. We cannot confirm the rules for every job, so ask Midvale Building and Safety at 801-567-7200 before work begins.

If you are comparing quotes, ask each plumber who pulls the permit and who schedules the inspection. That one question separates careful work from shortcuts.
