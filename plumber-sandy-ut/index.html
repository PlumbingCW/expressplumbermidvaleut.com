Sandy overview

Plumbing in Sandy, from the old center to the newer streets

Sandy is a large city with several kinds of housing, and each one fails in its own way. Here is what we see most.

Emergency dispatch around the clock

Burst pipes, failed water heaters and sewage backups are answered 24/7. Nights, weekends and holidays are covered.

Older homes near the original center

Settled in the 1860s and 1870s, Sandy's oldest blocks may still have aged supply and drain pipe. A camera or pressure check shows what is actually underground.

Neighborhoods from the late 1960s onward

Sandy's second boom began in the late 1960s and spread outward. Much of that housing has copper supply lines and PVC or ABS drains, so shutoff valves and fittings that are 30 or more years old deserve a look.

Summer well water

Sandy City runs 16 wells that generally operate only in the summer. When the source mix changes, some homeowners notice pressure or taste shifts at the tap.

When a Sandy plumbing problem is an emergency

Plenty of plumbing trouble can wait until morning. These situations cannot, because water or sewage is damaging the house while you decide.

Call the 24/7 line right away if you see any of the following:

  • Water spraying or pouring from a pipe, a ceiling or a wall
  • A water heater leaking from the tank body, or rumbling and hissing loudly
  • Sewage or gray water coming up through a tub, shower or floor drain
  • A wet spot in the yard, or a water meter spinning with every tap closed
  • No water at all from a main line after a freeze

Before We Arrive

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak in Sandy

These four moves limit damage while our emergency plumber is on the way. None of them need tools.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main water shutoff clockwise until it stops. In many Sandy basements it sits near the water heater or where the line enters the house.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off the breaker only if you can reach it without stepping in water. Otherwise stay out and tell us.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics up and away from the leak. Keep kids and pets out of any room with standing water or sewage.

Call our 24/7 line

Tell us where the water is, what you shut off, and which fixtures still run. That lets us bring the right parts.

For a pipe that has already burst, our burst pipe repair page explains what we do on site. If you smell gas, leave the house and call Enbridge Gas at 1-800-767-1689 or 911 before you call us.

Local history

Smelters, railroads and a late-1960s boom: why it matters under your floor

Sandy was incorporated in 1893, after the railroad arrived in 1873 and the Flagstaff, Mingo and Saturn smelters turned the area into a working industrial town. Farming took over after the 1890s, and the city stayed small until its second boom began in the late 1960s.

That boom explains most of what we open up today. Housing from roughly the 1970s through the 1990s is the bulk of newer Sandy, and it falls in the era of copper supply and PVC or ABS drains. It also overlaps with the years gray polybutylene pipe was installed across the US, roughly the late 1970s to the mid-1990s, so a home from that stretch is worth a look at its supply lines.

Water heaters installed in those same decades are now at the age where tank failures turn up. If yours is leaking, our water heater repair team can tell you quickly whether a fix is realistic or the tank has reached the end.

Sandy City water, wells and what to do about lead

Sandy City water comes mostly from surface sources managed by the Metropolitan Water District of Salt Lake and Sandy, drawn from Little Cottonwood Creek, Bell Canyon Creek and the Provo River. The city adds 16 wells that generally only operate in the summer months. Sandy's 2021 water quality report publishes no hardness figure, so test your own tap if scale on fixtures worries you.

On lead, Sandy City says it mostly comes from service lines and home plumbing materials, and it advises flushing taps for 30 seconds to 2 minutes after water has sat for several hours. We can check what material your home plumbing uses, but the service line to the street is a question for Sandy City, not us.
